Opening range filter

Read each 9:30–9:35 candle high and low off the chart. Windows, strike band and stop price come out the other side. Everything you type is kept until you start a new day.

9:35 input

ADR carries over between days; the range and volume fields clear each morning. RVOL is optional: under 1.5 the name isn’t in play and gets flagged.

TickerADR %OR highOR lowRVOL

Before the order goes in

  • 0DTE. Premium $0.30–$0.60, five contracts.
  • Set the stop as an order when you enter, not as a decision for later.
  • Enter a candle after confirmation, not on the extension.
  • Marked daily level overhead? Check before a long.

Windows are the 30–70% band of one ADR from the opening-range extreme; strikes inside ±1 ADR; stop at −33% of fill. Drawn from 42 scored trades, 08/10–08/27.
